## Read old reports with their original assumptions

The public [October 19 research](2026-10-19/) is the board's provenance: the [board audit](2026-10-19/board-audit.md), the [news sweep](2026-10-19/news-sweep.md) behind the GP cuts, and [schedule counts](2026-10-19/schedule.md). The season-total objective, simulated survival percentages, automatic second-center target, and OUT-as-IR-stash advice were superseded. Extra NBA games provide more lock opportunities. They do not add multiple counted game scores for a starter in the same week. OUT players take a bench spot here.

News, positions, ranks, and opponent behavior can change. Verify a source's date and the live Sleeper data before using an archived claim. Historical simulation scripts and temporary-file instructions are not part of the supported draft workflow.
