# Lock-In backtest on 2025-26 Sleeper game logs

Run on October 19, 2026, the night before the season. It tests the board's Lock-In model (`rebuild_board.lock_values`) and measures how much bench rotation is worth. Scripts and raw outputs are in [`backtest/`](backtest/). To rerun from any clone, run `python3 q12.py`, `python3 q3.py 300` and `python3 q3_boot.py` in `backtest/` (about 2 minutes; `q3.py` defaults to 40 random rosters for a quick look). On Oct 20 a fresh clone reproduced `q12.out` and `q3.out` byte for byte; `q3_boot.out` was regenerated by the committed script, whose intervals differ from the first, unsaved bootstrap by at most 0.006. Missing 2025 and 2024 weekly logs download on first use from `https://api.sleeper.com/stats/nba/{season}/{week}?season_type=regular` into the gitignored `data/` and `data24/` folders. Q3 needs a Sleeper `/players/nba` snapshot for positions: set `BACKTEST_PLAYERS=<path>`, or it uses the newest `.cache/players-*.json` in the repo and downloads one if there is none. The published numbers used the 2026-10-19 snapshot.

**Bottom line**
- **Lock thresholds work.** Locking a score when it beats the value of waiting captures about 68% of the gap between "always take the last game" and perfect hindsight, worth about +50–70 FP/week over naive play.
- **Waiting is worth about 8% less than `lock_values` says.** The game-score distribution is well calibrated; the gap comes entirely from players sitting a game they were scheduled to play later in the week (DNPs). `gm.py lock` corrects for this.
- **Bench rotation is worth about +12–13 FP/week.** For trades, a bench player is worth about **0.60×** their board V, not the 0.25 that `trade.py` first assumed.

## Data and method

- 2025-26 logs scored with `fit_lockin.SCORING`, deduplicated by (player, game). This reproduces `fit_lockin.py`'s 151 regulars.
- A player's weekly schedule is every game his team played that week. A scheduled game with no `sp>0` row counts as a DNP.
- The causal FP/g prior comes from 2024-25 logs.
- Self-checks:
  - `lib.seq_value` equals `lock_values` when every remaining game has the same FP/g.
  - `q12.py` asserts on its policy runner.
  - In `q3.py`, rotation with a single player equals the fixed policy.

## Q1. Single-slot threshold policy

367 players with 40+ games; 8,149 player-weeks with at least one game played. A slot-week is one starting slot for one week.

| Policy | FP/slot-week | Share of last→hindsight gap |
|---|---:|---:|
| Always last game (0 if DNP) | 21.52 | 0% |
| Lock first game played | 24.18 | 22% |
| Threshold, season-mean FP/g | 29.73 | 69.0% |
| Threshold, running-mean FP/g (2024-25 prior, 10-game weight) | 29.45 | 66.6% |
| Threshold + DNP correction (q=0.08), season / running mean | 29.85 / 29.52 | 70.0% / 67.2% |
| Hindsight max | 33.42 | 100% |

Using a running mean instead of the true season mean costs only 0.3 FP per slot-week.

## Q2. Calibration and DNP risk

Realized ÷ predicted value of waiting, at every played game with games still left:

| Group | k=1 | k=2 | k=3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| All decision points | 0.903 | 0.915 | 0.921 |
| Regulars | 0.910 | 0.918 | 0.913 |
| Regulars, no later DNP | 0.996 | 1.006 | 1.008 |

There is no trend by FP/g (range 0.87–0.95). Whole weeks: predicted 32.4, realized 29.7.

Chance of missing the next in-week game after playing the previous one:

| Group | Rate |
|---|---:|
| All 40+ game players | 11.4% |
| Regulars, 25+ FP/g | 8.3% |
| Regulars with 65+ games | 6.3% |
| Next game is the second night of a back-to-back | 12.4% |
| At least one rest day before the next game | 7.0% |

These rates include absences announced before tip-off, so they are an upper bound on true surprises.

**Correction:** `V1 = (1−q)·E[X]` and `Vk = (1−q)·E[max(X, V(k−1))] + q·V(k−1)`, with q=0.08 (0.12 before a back-to-back second night).
- **As a lock rule:** it adds only about +0.1 FP per slot-week. Flat waiting-value multipliers from 0.90 to 0.97 all land within 0.15 FP of each other.
- **As a valuation:** it matters. Values built on `lock_values` run about 8% high. The bias is roughly uniform across players, so board ranks barely move. `board.txt` is unchanged; `gm.py lock` applies the correction.
- **Not checked:** how much of the 8% is already absorbed by `week_value`'s missed-games (gp/82) thinning.

## Q3. Bench rotation

**Rosters:**
- **Ours:** 13 drafted players have 2025-26 logs. Acuff has none, so Kon Knueppel (36.4 FP/g, SG/SF) stands in.
- **Random rosters (300):** one player from each 10-rank band of the top 140 by 2025-26 season FP.
- **Replacement level:** ranks 141–180, about 27 FP/wk standalone.

**Rotation policy:**
- The best 9 by weekly value get home slots.
- When a home player is idle that day, the best free bench player who plays fills his slot.
- Each slot locks when its score beats the optimal-stopping value of the slot's planned future rolls. The biggest gain locks first, and the plan is redone after each lock.
- The baseline is the same best 9, each locking alone with no rotation.

**Scenarios:** "Realistic" means whole-week absences are known in advance and in-week DNPs are surprises.

| Scenario (random rosters) | FP/wk |
|---|---:|
| Best 9, no bench | 395.8 |
| Best 9 set weekly from 14 | 423.2 |
| With rotation | 436.1 (+13.0; middle 80% +6.9 to +19.1) |

Our roster: 467.0 → 478.5 with rotation (+11.5).

Marginal value of roster spots 10–14: +17.6, +7.7, +6.1, +4.3, +3.8 FP/wk.

**Bench discount factor** (team gain ÷ the players' standalone weekly value), realistic scenario, 95% bootstrap CI:

| Measure | Factor | 95% CI |
|---|---:|---|
| All 5 bench vs empty spot | 0.253 | 0.245–0.262 |
| Top 3 bench vs empty spot | 0.332 | 0.321–0.345 |
| **Top 3 bench vs replacement (V basis)** | **0.595** | **0.53–0.67** |
| All 5 bench vs replacement | 0.493 | 0.44–0.54 |

On the V basis, the top-3 factor is 0.45 if in-week DNPs are never foreseen and 0.72 with perfect foresight. Our own roster measured lower (0.42 top 3), because its bench is guard-heavy and overlaps the starters. `trade.py` now uses 0.60. Its old 0.25 matched the "all 5 vs empty spot" measure, not V.

**Simulated check (issue #10).** `trade.sim_value` runs `gm.py week`'s model (base lineup, rotation plan, DNP-corrected stopping) over the 2026 schedule, weeks 2–20, with whole-week absences from the board's own `cluster × (1 − gp/82)`. On the same V-basis top-3 measure (`backtest/q3_sim.py 300`, needs the network; output in `q3_sim.out`): random board rosters **0.613** (95% CI 0.561–0.667), our roster **0.430**. Both match the measured 0.595 and 0.42. Without the absence term the simulation gives 0.46 and 0.29 (40 rosters, not saved): covering starters' missed weeks is most of a bench's value.

## Q4. Lock thresholds for the Owner

Lock if score ≥ multiple × FP/g. "Games left" means remaining games after the one just finished. Includes the q=0.08 DNP correction.

| FP/g | 1 left | 2 left | 3 left | 4 left |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 30 | 0.92 | 1.13 | 1.24 | 1.32 |
| 40 | 0.92 | 1.10 | 1.20 | 1.27 |
| 50 | 0.92 | 1.08 | 1.17 | 1.22 |
| 60 | 0.92 | 1.06 | 1.14 | 1.19 |
| 75 | 0.92 | 1.04 | 1.11 | 1.15 |

Rule of thumb: about 0.92× with 1 game left, 1.1× with 2, 1.2× with 3–4. Subtract about 0.03 if the next game is the second night of a back-to-back. If the next game is ruled out, count only the games after it. These multiples are for one player's slot; when a bench player can take the slot, `gm.py lock` compares against the slot's best continuation instead.

## Limits

- Q3 treats each player's season-mean FP/g as known all season.
- The logs have no tip times, so two games on the same date can't share a slot. This slightly understates rotation.
- An unlocked slot scores its last roll (0 on a DNP). Sleeper's exact rule after the occupant is swapped out is unverified.
- Planning is greedy: a bench player can appear in several slots' future plans. One overcounting bug was fixed; the plan is still approximate.
- Positions come from the 2026 player cache, applied to 2025-26 schedules.
- The random-roster bench is weaker than ours (median 28.4 FP/g against our 30–37). Waiver streaming is not modeled.
